## Reporting a Vulnerability

Restudio Software Laboratories accepts security vulnerability reports through its dedicated support email address.

**Security Reporting Email:** restudiosupport@gmail.com

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities privately and do not disclose unpatched vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues or discussions.

Where possible, include the following information in your report:

- A clear description of the vulnerability.
- The affected operating system, package, or component.
- The affected version.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.
- The potential security impact.
- Suggested mitigations or fixes, if available.

Please do not include passwords, access tokens, private signing keys, or unrelated personal information in your report.

Reports will be reviewed by the project maintainers. Response times may vary depending on the severity and complexity of the issue.

Submitting a report does not guarantee a particular response time or disclosure schedule. Restudio Software Laboratories will assess the report and determine the appropriate steps for investigation, remediation, and disclosure.
